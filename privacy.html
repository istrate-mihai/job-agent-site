Privacy Policy — job-agent

Last updated: 2026-09-24

Data accessed

job-agent requests the gmail.readonly scope. It reads email messages in the authorizing user's Gmail account to identify job-application correspondence. It never sends, modifies, or deletes email.

Storage

OAuth tokens and any processed data are stored only on the owner's local machine. No data is sent to third-party servers operated by the developer.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared, or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
